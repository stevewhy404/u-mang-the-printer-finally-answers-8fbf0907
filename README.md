One line, in the office printer's own voice, as its comeback to the paper jams, the cryptic error codes, and the thumps when it stops working.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (1–240 characters, UTF-8), deterministically — the same line on every run.

> I jammed once in 40,000 pages and got thumped for it; you jam every meeting and get a promotion.
